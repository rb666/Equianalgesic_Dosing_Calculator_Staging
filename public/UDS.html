Why use this UDS tool?

A concise overview of how the tool improves UDS review compared with fragmented manual lookup.

Structured workflow instead of fragmented lookup

This UDS reference and reconciliation tool turns a traditionally fragmented review process into a single structured workflow. Instead of manually cross-checking medication lists, metabolites, assay limitations, panel coverage, detection windows, false-positive risks, and confirmatory testing needs across multiple references, clinicians can work through one guided interface.

Faster clinical answers

Lookup answers focused questions such as whether fentanyl appears on a routine opiate screen, whether 7-aminoclonazepam supports clonazepam exposure, or why hydromorphone may appear with hydrocodone.

Real report reconciliation

Pattern Check lets the user enter expected medications, detected findings, and only confirmed tested-absent analytes, then organizes the output around method/panel coverage, compatibility, source ambiguity, absent-finding risk, and next steps.

Fewer missed pitfalls

The tool highlights when a finding is compatible, unexpected, assay-dependent, panel-dependent, or not source-specific by itself. This is especially useful for shared metabolites such as hydromorphone, oxymorphone, morphine, oxazepam, temazepam, and nordiazepam.

Cleaner documentation

Copyable summaries support faster chart review, pharmacist review, consult notes, and communication around unexpected positives, unexpected negatives, and metabolite ambiguity.

Workflow impact

The practical impact is less time spent searching separate references, more consistent interpretation between reviewers, more cautious handling of presumptive immunoassay results, and clearer identification of when definitive testing or lab consultation is appropriate. The tool helps reduce premature conclusions about adherence, misuse, diversion, timing, or impairment when the urine result alone cannot support those conclusions.
